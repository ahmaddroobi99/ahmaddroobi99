Conceptual Notes

Relativity and Spacetime

  • Galilean Relativity: time absolute; space Euclidean.
  • Newtonian Absolute Space: fixed spatial frame.
  • Three-Velocity: \( \mathbf{v} \) lacks time component.

Thermodynamics and Information

  • Negentropy: “distance” from maximum entropy.
  • Non-equilibrium: sustained fluxes; \( \nabla \cdot \mathbf{J}\neq0 \).
  • Deterministic Information: zero uncertainty signals.

Probability and Decision Theory

  • Dirac Delta: certain outcome \( \delta(x-x_0) \).
  • Frequentist: probability as long-run frequency.
  • Disutility: cost-centric \( U(x)\le 0 \).

Complex Systems

  • Reductionism: analyze parts, reassemble whole.
  • Linear Dynamics: \( y=mx+c \); superposition holds.
  • Static Equilibrium: no net change in state variables.

ALGORITHM

Hybrid EnKF–PF for Barotropic QG Flow with Lagrangian Tracers

Input: \( \{\mathbf{x}_k^0\}_{k=1}^{N} \), velocity field, tracer paths \( \mathbf{X} \), \( \Delta t \), \( \mathbf{R} \)
Output: Posterior mean \( \mathbf{m}^a(t) \) at saved times \( t_s \)
  1. Initialize spectral domain \( \mathbf{k}_x,\mathbf{k}_y,\lVert\mathbf{k}\rVert \); damping \( \gamma(\mathbf{k}) \); noise \( \sigma(\mathbf{k}) \).
  2. Build \( \mathcal{H}(\mathbf{X}) \) via \( q\to \psi \to (u,v) \).
  3. For \( t=1..T \) do
    Forecast (all ensemble members)
    For \( k=1..N \) do
    \( \psi_k \gets \mathcal{F}^{-1}\!\left[ \hat{q}_k / (\lVert\mathbf{k}\rVert^2 + \mu) \right] \)
    Compute nonlinear advection \( \partial q_k/\partial t \) via pseudo-spectral QG.
    \( \mathbf{x}_k^{f} \gets \mathbf{x}_k^{f} + \Delta t\, \partial q_k/\partial t + \Sigma \boldsymbol{\xi}_k \)
    end for
    Analysis on save cadence
    If \( \mathrm{mod}(t,n_{\text{save}})=0 \):
    Construct observation matrix \( \mathbf{H} \) from tracer locations.
    Ingest observed velocities \( \mathbf{d}(t) \) from \( \dot{\mathbf{X}} \).
    Predict obs: \( \mathbf{Y}_k=\mathbf{H}\mathbf{x}_k^{f} \).
    \[ \mathbf{P}_{yy}=\frac{1}{N-1}\!\sum(\mathbf{Y}_k-\bar{\mathbf{Y}})(\mathbf{Y}_k-\bar{\mathbf{Y}})^\top + \mathbf{R},\quad \mathbf{P}_{xy}=\frac{1}{N-1}\!\sum(\mathbf{x}_k^{f}-\bar{\mathbf{x}})(\mathbf{Y}_k-\bar{\mathbf{Y}})^\top \]
    \[ \mathbf{K}=\mathbf{P}_{xy}\mathbf{P}_{yy}^{-1} \]
    EnKF update
    For \( k=1..N \) do
    \( \mathbf{x}_k^{a}\gets \mathbf{x}_k^{f}+\mathbf{K}(\mathbf{d}-\mathbf{Y}_k) \)
    end for
    PF reweight + resample
    \[ w_k \propto \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{d}-\mathbf{Y}_k)^\top \mathbf{R}^{-1}(\mathbf{d}-\mathbf{Y}_k)\right), \quad w_k \leftarrow \frac{w_k}{\sum_j w_j} \]
    Resample \( \{\mathbf{x}_k^{a}\} \leftarrow \mathrm{Resample}(\{\mathbf{x}_k^{a}\},\{w_k\}) \)
    Store mean \( \mathbf{m}(t)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^N \mathbf{x}_k^{a} \)
ALGORITHM

Systematic Resampling of Ensemble Members

Input: Weighted ensemble \( \{ \mathbf{x}_k, w_k \}_{k=1}^{N} \)
Output: Resampled ensemble \( \{ \tilde{\mathbf{x}}_k \}_{k=1}^{N} \)
  1. Compute cumulative weights \( W_k=\sum_{j=1}^k w_j \).
  2. Set stride \( \delta=1/N \).
  3. Draw \( u\sim \mathcal{U}[0,\delta] \).
  4. Initialize \( i\leftarrow1 \).
  5. For \( k=1..N \) do
    \( u_k=u+(k-1)\delta \)
    while \( u_k>W_i \): \( i\leftarrow i+1 \)
    Set \( \tilde{\mathbf{x}}_k\leftarrow \mathbf{x}_i \)
    end for

Research Proposal: Information-Theoretic Bayesian Optimization

Unifying Bayesian Optimization and Data Assimilation for complex systems.

The Scientific Challenge

Realistic simulators are expensive; informative experiments are scarce. We select actions that maximize learning under cost constraints.

Core Optimization Problem

\[ \boldsymbol{\pi}^*=\arg\max_{\boldsymbol{\pi}\in\Pi} \mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{\pi}}\!\left[I(\mathbf{x}_t;\mathbf{y}_t\mid\mathcal{D}_{1:t-1})-\lambda C(\boldsymbol{\pi})\right] \]

Information-Theoretic Framework

Pose a joint design/assimilation step that directly trades off information gain and feasibility.

Information Gain

\[ I(\mathbf{x};\mathbf{y}\mid\mathcal{D})=\mathrm{KL}\!\left( p(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}\mid\mathcal{D})\;\big\|\; p(\mathbf{x}\mid\mathcal{D})\,p(\mathbf{y}\mid\mathcal{D}) \right) \]
