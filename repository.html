Conceptual Notes

Relativity and Spacetime

  • Galilean Relativity: Framework where time is absolute and space is Euclidean
  • Newtonian Absolute Space: Non-relativistic framework with fixed spatial coordinates
  • Three-Velocity: Classical velocity vector without time component \( \mathbf{v} \)

Thermodynamics and Information

  • Negentropy: Measure of distance from thermodynamic equilibrium \( S_{\text{neg}} \)
  • Non-equilibrium Thermodynamics: Systems with energy/matter flows
  • Deterministic Information: Perfectly predictable signals with zero uncertainty

Probability and Decision Theory

  • Dirac Delta Function: Represents definite, certain outcomes \( \delta(x) \)
  • Frequentist Inference: Probability as long-run frequency rather than belief
  • Disutility Function: Measures costs rather than benefits \( U(x) \)

Complex Systems

  • Reductionism: Understanding systems through constituent parts
  • Linear Dynamics: Systems with proportional cause-effect relationships \( y = mx + c \)
  • Static Equilibrium: Systems without evolutionary change

ALGORITHM

Hybrid EnKF–PF for Barotropic QG Flow with Lagrangian Tracers

Input: Initial ensemble \( \{\mathbf{x}_k^0\}_{k=1}^{N} \), velocity field, tracer paths \( \mathbf{X} \), time step \( \Delta t \), observation error covariance \( \mathbf{R} \)
Output: Posterior ensemble mean \( \mathbf{m}^a(t) \) at saved times \( t_s \)
  1. Initialize spectral domain \( \mathbf{k}_x,\mathbf{k}_y,\lVert\mathbf{k}\rVert \); damping \( \gamma(\mathbf{k}) \); noise \( \sigma(\mathbf{k}) \).
  2. Generate observation operator \( \mathcal{H}(\mathbf{X}) \) via velocity projections \( q \rightarrow \psi \rightarrow (u,v) \).
  3. For \( t = 1 \) to \( T \) do
    Forecast step over ensemble members
    For \( k = 1 \) to \( N \) do
    \( \psi_k \gets \mathcal{F}^{-1}\!\left[ \dfrac{\hat{q}_k}{\lVert\mathbf{k}\rVert^2 + \mu} \right] \)
    Compute nonlinear advection \( \dfrac{\partial q_k}{\partial t} \) via pseudo-spectral QG.
    \( \mathbf{x}_k^{f} \gets \mathbf{x}_k^{f} + \Delta t \, \dfrac{\partial q_k}{\partial t} + \Sigma \boldsymbol{\xi}_k \)
    end for
    Analysis step on save cadence
    If \( \mathrm{mod}(t,n_{\text{save}})=0 \) then:
    Construct observation matrix \( \mathbf{H} \) from tracer locations.
    Ingest observed velocities \( \mathbf{d}(t) \) from \( \dot{\mathbf{X}} \).
    Predict obs: \( \mathbf{Y}_k = \mathbf{H}\mathbf{x}_k^{f} \).
    \[ \mathbf{P}_{yy} = \frac{1}{N-1} \sum (\mathbf{Y}_k-\bar{\mathbf{Y}})(\mathbf{Y}_k-\bar{\mathbf{Y}})^\top + \mathbf{R}, \quad \mathbf{P}_{xy} = \frac{1}{N-1} \sum (\mathbf{x}_k^{f}-\bar{\mathbf{x}})(\mathbf{Y}_k-\bar{\mathbf{Y}})^\top \]
    \[ \mathbf{K} = \mathbf{P}_{xy}\mathbf{P}_{yy}^{-1} \]
    EnKF update
    For \( k = 1 \) to \( N \) do
    \( \mathbf{x}_k^{a} \gets \mathbf{x}_k^{f} + \mathbf{K}(\mathbf{d}-\mathbf{Y}_k) \)
    end for
    Particle filter reweight + resample
    \[ w_k \propto \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{d}-\mathbf{Y}_k)^\top \mathbf{R}^{-1}(\mathbf{d}-\mathbf{Y}_k)\right), \quad w_k \leftarrow \frac{w_k}{\sum_j w_j} \]
    Resample \( \{\mathbf{x}_k^{a}\} \leftarrow \mathrm{Resample}(\{\mathbf{x}_k^{a}\},\{w_k\}) \)
    Store posterior mean \( \mathbf{m}(t) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^N \mathbf{x}_k^{a} \)
    end for
ALGORITHM

Systematic Resampling of Ensemble Members

Input: Weighted ensemble \( \{ \mathbf{x}_k, w_k \}_{k=1}^{N} \)
Output: Resampled ensemble \( \{ \tilde{\mathbf{x}}_k \}_{k=1}^{N} \)
  1. Compute cumulative weights \( W_k = \sum_{j=1}^{k} w_j \).
  2. Set stride \( \delta = \frac{1}{N} \).
  3. Draw \( u \sim \mathcal{U}[0,\delta] \).
  4. Initialize \( i \leftarrow 1 \).
  5. For \( k = 1 \) to \( N \) do
    \( u_k = u + (k-1)\delta \)
    While \( u_k > W_i \): set \( i \leftarrow i+1 \)
    Set \( \tilde{\mathbf{x}}_k \leftarrow \mathbf{x}_i \)
    end for
