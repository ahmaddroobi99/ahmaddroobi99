About Me

I recently completed the MSc in Mechanical Engineering (Computational Applied Mathematics) at the University of Calgary, working in the Predictive & Intelligent Systems Lab. My research operationalizes Scientific Machine Learning and Bayesian inference for turbulent, stochastic flow fields.

Core focus areas include state/parameter estimation, uncertainty quantification, and data assimilation with sparse, noisy Lagrangian observations. In my thesis, I developed a hybrid Ensemble Kalman Filter–Particle Filter framework to reconstruct quasi-geostrophic flow fields and recover Eulerian energy spectra from tracer data in high-dimensional regimes (>16k states).

I’m motivated by decision-making under uncertainty across climate, environmental, and engineering systems, with emphasis on scalable numerics, reproducible pipelines, and HPC-ready implementations in Python/MATLAB.
